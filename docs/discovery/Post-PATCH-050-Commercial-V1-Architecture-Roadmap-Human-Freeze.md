## 2. Commercial V1 product definition

SATCO Commercial V1 is a Multi-Discipline-ready, AI-assisted Engineering
Intelligence and Decision-Support Platform. It is not merely a document
generator. AI remains advisory and non-authoritative. Human engineering
authority remains explicit.

One shared Core supports modular Discipline Packages without source, Core or
database forks. The commercially operational V1 packages are exactly:

1. Electrical;
2. Instrumentation;
3. Control & Automation.

Cross-Discipline Intelligence among these three packages is mandatory.

## 5. Discipline Package and cross-discipline freeze

The shared package contract must allow later governed Discipline Packages
without redesigning or forking Core identity, Project, Workspace, Context,
Evidence, Reports, Memory, Audit, Human authority or the AI gateway.

Electrical, Instrumentation and Control & Automation must be real operational
V1 packages, not labels or placeholders. Supported combinations include each
single package, any accepted combination and integrated E/I/C.

Cross-Discipline Intelligence must provide evidence-linked, explainable and
authorization-safe interfaces, consistency, missing-information, dependency,
change-impact and Guidance behavior among the three packages. It must not
autonomously resolve conflicts, mutate source facts or imply engineering
approval.

## 6. Methods & Systems / Engineering Performance freeze

Methods & Systems / Engineering Performance Intelligence is mandatory and
discipline-neutral. It initially consumes authorized Electrical,
Instrumentation, Control & Automation and shared engineering-management facts.

It may provide explainable engineering-process indicators, performance trends,
Engineering Health factors and advisory next actions. Every result must expose
its scope, source basis, calculation, time window, limitations and authorized
drill-down. It must not become HR ranking, employee surveillance, finance, ERP
or opaque personnel scoring.

## 7. Standards freeze

Commercial V1 Standards-Aware Technical Report Intelligence requires:

- standards and edition registry;
- applicability, current standing and supersession;
- rights metadata;
- governed retrieval adapters;
- immutable retrieval digest/snapshot identity;
- exact citation handles;
- Human selection;
- fail-closed citation validation;
- Technical Report integration;
- preservation of accepted citation identity in Organizational Memory.

AI must not invent standards, editions or clauses. Unauthorized wholesale
copyrighted standards content must not be stored or reproduced.

The architecture is publisher-neutral and supports multiple governed sources
subject to legal rights and availability. PATCH-054 design and deployment
configuration may select the first concrete sources without changing this
roadmap architecture.

## 8. Evidence freeze

Commercial V1 ordinary-user Evidence includes Evidence creation, safe
Supporting File linkage, provenance review, standing/lifecycle transitions,
supersession/withdrawal, lineage, Report-reliance visibility,
protected/unavailable states, minimum retention governance, hold flag,
disposition eligibility and recovery/export behavior.

Commercial V1 does not require a generic enterprise EDMS, broad records
management, OCR, semantic extraction or document collaboration.

## 9. Authentication and application-security freeze

Commercial V1 requires:

- mandatory administrator MFA using at minimum TOTP plus recovery codes;
- supported, Organization-configurable engineer/member MFA;
- short-lived access tokens;
- rotating hashed refresh sessions and reuse detection;
- explicit logout and session revocation;
- authentication throttling/backoff without account-existence leakage;
- bounded administrator-mediated account recovery;
- safe authentication and security Audit;
- complete tenant-isolation negative evidence.

Engineer/member MFA is not globally mandatory unless a later accepted security
review establishes that requirement. Enterprise SSO, SAML, OIDC and SCIM are
post-V1 unless separately re-baselined.

## 10. Deployment and operations freeze

Commercial V1 supports one official SATCO-managed dedicated single-customer
deployment profile with:

- TLS edge, frontend and FastAPI backend;
- PostgreSQL;
- private S3-compatible object storage and malware scanning;
- secrets management and separated database principals;
- encrypted off-host backup and isolated restore;
- upgrade, rollback and recovery procedures;
- monitoring and external alerts;
- support and break-glass procedures;
- clean signed release provenance;
- performed deployment, capacity and recovery evidence.

Kubernetes, HA, multi-region, large-scale SaaS orchestration and enterprise
federation are outside Commercial V1.

No SLO, RPO, RTO or capacity value is fabricated by this freeze. PATCH-060 must
measure supported values through representative exercises, and explicit Human
Commercial V1 Release Certification must accept/freeze them.

## 11. Entitlement freeze

Commercial V1 uses **signed offline entitlements** for the supported dedicated
deployment profile. Entitlements must support Core plus Electrical,
Instrumentation and Control & Automation individually and in supported
combinations, including integrated E/I/C.

The accepted design must bind Organization/deployment, enabled packages, seats,
validity, grace, support/update rights and signature as appropriate. Backend
enforcement fails closed and resists rollback. Expiry behavior preserves
authorized historical read/export according to the accepted design.

SaaS billing, payment processing, usage metering and advanced licensing
analytics are outside Commercial V1.
