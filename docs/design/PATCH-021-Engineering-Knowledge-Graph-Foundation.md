# PATCH-021 Engineering Knowledge Graph Foundation

## Status

Planned

## Objective

Establish the architectural foundation for the SATCO Engineering Knowledge
Graph (EKG).

The Engineering Knowledge Graph becomes the central engineering knowledge
model of SATCO Platform.

Artificial Intelligence shall reason on top of the Engineering Knowledge
Graph rather than directly on engineering documents.

## Mission

SATCO shall understand engineering objects instead of merely storing files.

Engineering Objects become the primary entities of the platform.

## Architectural Principles

- Engineering Object-Centric Architecture
- Knowledge before Intelligence
- Context before AI
- Human Judgment remains authoritative
- Engineering Knowledge Graph before Engineering Digital Twin

## Scope of PATCH-021

PATCH-021 is limited to the foundation of the Engineering Knowledge Graph.

This patch shall define:

- Engineering Object philosophy
- Engineering Object taxonomy
- Engineering relationship vocabulary
- Context boundaries
- Knowledge Graph architecture

PATCH-021 shall not implement:

- Engineering Digital Twin
- AI reasoning
- LLM workflows
- semantic search
- vector database
- recommendation engines

Those capabilities shall be considered only after the Engineering Knowledge
Graph reaches architectural maturity.

## Related Documents

- ADR-018 Engineering Intelligence Product Vision

## Exit Criteria

PATCH-021 is complete only after the Engineering Knowledge Graph foundation
is fully documented and approved by the Product Owner before implementation
begins.
