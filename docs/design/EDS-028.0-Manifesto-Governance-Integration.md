## 3. Engineering Objective

Make Manifesto alignment explicit, cumulative, reviewable, and blocking across
the complete SATCO delivery chain without changing the Manifesto's wording,
the hierarchy of Human authority, completed PATCH decisions, or product/backend
behavior.

## 4. Design Principles

### 4.1 One alignment record, progressively refined

Each PATCH begins one Manifesto Alignment Record. Later artifacts refine and
verify the same record rather than independently inventing unrelated compliance
claims.

The record contains:

- Manifesto version and source path;
- supported and affected principles;
- Engineering Intelligence contribution;
- known tensions, risks, and limitations;
- evidence references by lifecycle artifact;
- current result and accountable reviewer.

### 4.2 All principles remain binding

An artifact may identify principles that are directly affected and principles
that are preserved without new behavior. It may not declare a Manifesto
principle itself inapplicable. A technical gate may be non-applicable only when
reviewed design proves that the technical concern is absent.

### 4.3 PASS requires reasoning and evidence

A checkbox, template field, or generated statement is insufficient. PASS must
name the affected principles, explain the architecture/behavior that supports
them, identify conflicts or limitations, and reference the governing artifacts.

### 4.4 Human authority is unchanged

Codex and other AI tools may prepare, inspect, and technically review alignment
evidence. They cannot approve on behalf of the Product Owner, Architecture
Guardian, engineering authority, security authority, experience authority, or
Repository Owner.

### 4.5 Prospective adoption

QG-M1 applies to PATCH-028.0 and future PATCHes after its governed adoption.
PATCH-023 through PATCH-027 remain completed under their original authority and
are not retroactively reopened. If a completed capability is materially changed
later, its new PATCH follows QG-M1.

## 5. Manifesto Alignment Record

The canonical logical contract is:

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Manifesto reference | Exact path and certified version |
| Supported principles | Principles the change positively advances |
| Affected principles | Principles whose preservation requires a design decision |
| Preserved principles | Principles reviewed with no new semantic effect |
| Engineering Intelligence contribution | Real engineering value and trust improvement |
| Tensions and risks | Tradeoffs, uncertainty, or possible principle conflict |
| Evidence map | Artifact sections and validation evidence |
| Result | PENDING, PASS, or FAIL |
| Reviewer/authority | Technical reviewer and required Human decision owner |
| Review date | Attributable date of current result |

Documents may render the record as Markdown sections or tables. Version 1.1
does not introduce a database or machine-readable manifest.

## 6. Artifact Behavior

### 6.1 PATCH

Every new PATCH or sub-PATCH shall:

1. reference the certified Manifesto version;
2. identify supported, affected, and preserved principles;
3. explain the Engineering Intelligence contribution;
4. record known tensions and risks;
5. include alignment acceptance criteria;
6. prohibit implementation when a conflict is unresolved.

The PATCH establishes the initial record. It cannot certify its own final
compliance.

### 6.2 Architecture Review

Every AR shall independently test:

- consistency with all eleven principles;
- whether the claimed engineering value is real and within Version-1 scope;
- preservation of Human authority, context, evidence, explainability,
  organizational ownership, and provider independence;
- whether one principle was satisfied by weakening another;
- conflicts, conditions, and required design work.

The AR outputs `Manifesto Compliance: PASS` or `FAIL`. A conditional technical
recommendation is not PASS until blocking conditions are closed and re-reviewed.

### 6.3 EDS

EDS maps affected principles to required engineering behavior, ownership,
lifecycle, responsibility, context, evidence, and failure semantics. It closes
architecture decisions delegated by the PATCH and AR without prescribing code.

### 6.4 EDS Review

The independent EDS Review verifies that every affected principle has complete
behavioral coverage and no lower-level design can bypass the intended trust
boundary. `FAIL` returns the work to EDS or earlier architecture.

### 6.5 IDS

IDS maps each approved Manifesto behavior to exact files, contracts, tests, and
documentation edits. Every authorized file must trace to one PATCH/EDS
deliverable. IDS cannot reinterpret the Manifesto or broaden PATCH scope.

For documentation-only PATCH-028.0, IDS shall provide exact section-level edit
instructions, terminology, cross-reference behavior, and validation checks.

### 6.6 Implementation Plan

The plan includes a Manifesto checkpoint for every Sprint, a final diff review,
cross-document consistency checks, rollback/restoration steps, and stop
conditions for conflicts or unauthorized files.

### 6.7 IRR

IRR verifies the completed chain and states exactly:

```text
Manifesto Alignment Verified: YES | NO
QG-M1 Readiness Result: PASS | FAIL
```

`NO` or `FAIL` prohibits `READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION`. IRR does not repeat the
entire analysis; it references the accepted evidence chain and confirms that
current repository assumptions still hold.

### 6.8 Sprint checkpoints

Every Sprint checkpoint verifies:

- changed files remain within IDS authority;
- implementation behavior still matches affected principles;
- no new assumption, provider coupling, hidden authority, context loss, or
  evidence weakening was introduced;
- new tension returns work to the earliest affected documentation phase.

### 6.9 Final Review

Final Review reruns QG-M1 against the actual diff, validation evidence, and
runtime behavior. Design-time PASS does not automatically prove final PASS.

## 7. QG-M1 State Model

### 7.1 States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PENDING | Required evidence or Human decision is incomplete |
| PASS | All principles were reviewed; affected principles have supported evidence; no unresolved conflict remains |
| FAIL | A conflict, missing evidence, weakened principle, or false authority claim exists |

`PENDING` is not equivalent to PASS and cannot support readiness or completion.

### 7.2 Readiness evaluation

QG-M1 readiness PASS requires:

- approved PATCH alignment record;
- AR Manifesto Compliance PASS;
- accepted EDS and independent review PASS;
- approved IDS with traceability to exact files/contracts/tests;
- executable plan with checkpoints and stop conditions;
- IRR verification `YES`;
- every required Human approval recorded;
- current repository state compatible with the approved chain.

### 7.3 Final evaluation

QG-M1 Final PASS requires:

- exact final diff within IDS scope;
- validation evidence for every affected principle;
- no unresolved warning that changes engineering meaning or trust;
- independent Final Review PASS;
- no AI-generated or automated result represented as Human approval.

### 7.4 Failure routing

| Failure | Return point |
|---|---|
| Foundation conflict | Governance/Foundation review |
| Missing or invalid ownership decision | Architecture/ADR |
| Incomplete behavior | EDS |
| Missing exact contract/file/test | IDS |
| Repository mismatch | Readiness/IDS reassessment |
| Implementation divergence | Earliest affected Sprint/design phase |
| Final evidence gap | Validation/Final Review |

## 8. Framework Integration Design

### Framework Constitution

Add Manifesto alignment to non-negotiable principles, Framework authority, and
mandatory invariants. Do not change role authority or completion semantics.

### Implementation Workflow

Add Manifesto resolution to Phase A, record verification to the mandatory
governance flow, Sprint checks, and final reporting. Preserve existing Phase
ordering and QG-1 through QG-12.

### Sprint Engine

Make the approved alignment record a Sprint input and add a QG-M1 checkpoint to
Sprint DONE criteria. Do not create a new Sprint class.

### Validation Engine

Define reproducible alignment evidence and correction routing. Do not allow
self-attested PASS without source artifacts.

### Codex Runtime

Require resolution of the Manifesto and approved alignment chain, include
QG-M1 in runtime architecture checks and reports, and stop on unresolved
conflict. Codex still cannot approve architecture or Human decisions.

### Quality Gates

Define QG-M1 as a named cumulative gate evaluated at readiness and Final
Review. Preserve QG-0 through QG-12 identifiers and meanings to avoid breaking
existing completion records.

## 9. Governance and Lifecycle Integration Design

The Governance Model shall:

- register PATCH-028.0 only after the required authority accepts its number and
  scope;
- state that execution artifacts must contain Manifesto evidence;
- preserve the existing Foundation v1.2 hierarchy and ownership;
- state prospective adoption and completed-PATCH compatibility.

The Development Lifecycle shall add the appropriate Manifesto record/output to
PATCH, AR, EDS, EDS Review, IDS, IRR, Sprint/Implementation, Validation, and
Final Review phases without reordering or skipping any phase.

The documentation guide shall add navigation only; it shall not create new
authority.

## 10. Failure and Conflict Semantics

- Missing alignment record: governance failure, `NOT READY`.
- AR Manifesto Compliance FAIL: architecture blocker.
- EDS/IDS disagreement with the accepted record: contract blocker.
- IRR verification NO or QG-M1 FAIL: readiness prohibited.
- implementation violates alignment: work stops and returns to the earliest
  affected phase.
- Foundation conflict: lower-level work stops; Foundation authority governs.
- reviewer disagreement: no silent reconciliation; Governance resolves it.

2. readiness and Final Review both require QG-M1 PASS;
3. `PENDING` and `FAIL` cannot produce READY, COMPLETE, or DONE;
4. all eleven Manifesto principles remain intact;
5. Constitution supremacy and Human approval authority remain intact;
6. QG-0 through QG-12 and lifecycle ordering remain unchanged;
7. completed PATCHes are explicitly grandfathered without reopening;
8. all internal paths and cross-references resolve;
9. no backend or non-authorized file changes;
10. `git diff --check` passes;
11. independent Final Review passes.

## 13. Explicit Non-Scope

- modification of the Constitution or Manifesto text;
- product, backend, frontend, schema, migration, API, test, configuration, or
  infrastructure changes;
- machine-readable compliance storage or enforcement software;
- automatic approval or AI self-certification;
- changing Framework roles, states, lifecycle order, or QG-0 through QG-12;
- retroactive recertification of completed PATCHes;
- ADR-021 acceptance or PATCH-028 product design;
- commit, push, release, or deployment.
