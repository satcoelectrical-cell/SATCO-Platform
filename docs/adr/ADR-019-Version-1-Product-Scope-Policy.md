# ADR-019 Version-1 Product Scope Policy

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Version 1 of SATCO Platform is intended to establish and operate SATCO
Engineering before broader commercial expansion.

The objective is not to build a complete enterprise platform.

The objective is to create the best Engineering Intelligence Platform for
Electrical, Instrumentation, and Industrial Automation engineers.

Future commercial modules shall be added only after Version 1 successfully
supports the real daily engineering activities of SATCO Engineering.

## Decision

Version 1 shall remain intentionally focused.

No capability shall be implemented simply because it could be useful in the
future.

Every capability must directly improve the daily engineering workflow of
SATCO Engineering.

## Version 1 Scope

Version 1 includes only:

- Electrical Engineering
- Instrumentation Engineering
- Industrial Automation
- Technical Procurement
- Vendor Technical Proposal Review
- Engineering Context
- Engineering Intelligence

## Explicit Non-Scope

Version 1 shall not attempt to replace:

- ERP systems;
- Primavera;
- SAP;
- CMMS platforms;
- Enterprise Workflow platforms;
- Generic BPM systems;
- Enterprise HR systems;
- Financial systems.

Version 1 shall not attempt to become an all-in-one enterprise platform.

## Product Acceptance Rule

Version 1 is considered successful only if SATCO Engineering can perform its
real daily engineering work using SATCO Platform.

Commercial expansion shall occur after this objective is achieved.

## Feature Acceptance Policy

A new feature may enter Version 1 only if:

- it solves a real engineering problem;
- it improves engineering productivity;
- it supports Electrical, Instrumentation, or Automation engineering;
- it aligns with the Engineering Intelligence vision;
- it is explicitly approved by the Product Owner.

Otherwise, the feature shall be deferred to a future roadmap.

## Long-Term Strategy

The architecture shall remain open for future modules including:

- Maintenance;
- Methods & Systems;
- HSE;
- Operations;
- Reliability;
- Asset Integrity;
- Quality;
- additional engineering domains.

Architectural readiness does not imply implementation priority.

## Product Principle

Build depth before breadth.

Engineering value before feature count.

Daily engineering workflow before enterprise expansion.
