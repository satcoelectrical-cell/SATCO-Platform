# ADR-018 Engineering Intelligence Product Vision

## Status

Accepted

## Context

SATCO is not intended to replace engineers.

SATCO is not intended to replace Engineering Management Systems.

SATCO is not intended to automatically generate engineering documents
without engineering judgement.

Instead, SATCO is designed to become the Engineering Intelligence layer
that assists engineers throughout the engineering lifecycle.

The primary customer of Version 1 is SATCO Engineering itself.

The platform shall therefore solve real daily engineering problems before
expanding into broader enterprise capabilities.

## Decision

SATCO shall be developed as an Engineering Intelligence Platform.

Engineering Intelligence means:

- understanding engineering context;
- understanding project history;
- understanding vendor documentation;
- understanding engineering standards;
- understanding engineering decisions;
- providing engineering recommendations;
- identifying engineering risks;
- improving engineering productivity.

SATCO shall never replace engineering judgement.

Final engineering responsibility always remains with the engineer.

## Product Philosophy

SATCO works beside engineers.

SATCO does not replace engineers.

SATCO complements existing enterprise systems.

SATCO is intended to become the engineer's daily working companion.

## Version 1 Scope

Version 1 focuses exclusively on:

- Electrical Engineering
- Instrumentation Engineering
- Industrial Automation
- Technical Procurement
- Engineering Context
- Engineering Intelligence

Everything outside this scope shall require explicit Product Owner approval.

## Product Rules

Every new capability must satisfy all of the following:

- solve a real engineering problem;
- improve engineering productivity;
- preserve engineering judgement;
- align with Version 1 priorities;
- be approved by the Product Owner.

Features that violate these principles shall not become part of SATCO.

## Non-Goals

SATCO is not:

- Primavera;
- SAP;
- an ERP;
- a CMMS;
- a generic workflow platform;
- a one-click engineering document generator.

## Guiding Principles

Engineer in Control.

AI Assists — Never Replaces.

Engineering Judgement First.

Solve Real Engineering Problems.

Version 1 Before Expansion.

## Vision Statement

SATCO shall become the indispensable Engineering Intelligence Platform
for Electrical, Instrumentation, and Automation engineers.
