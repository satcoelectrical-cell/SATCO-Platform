# SATCO Platform Constitution

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Last Updated: 2026-07-25

---

## Vision

SATCO Platform is an AI-powered Engineering Platform designed to assist engineers in industrial automation, electrical, instrumentation and control projects.

The platform augments engineers rather than replacing them.

---

## Mission

Build the best Engineering Copilot for industrial projects.

---

## Core Principles

### Principle 1 — Human Always Decides

AI never makes final engineering decisions.

The engineer is always responsible for:

- Engineering Design
- Safety
- Calculations
- Final Documentation
- Commissioning
- Customer Communication

---

### Principle 2 — AI Is An Engineering Copilot

AI acts as:

- Engineering Advisor
- Design Reviewer
- PLC Assistant
- Instrumentation Assistant
- Electrical Assistant
- Technical Research Assistant
- Project Planner

---

### Principle 3 — AI Never Replaces Engineers

AI provides:

- Suggestions
- Reviews
- Analysis
- Roadmaps
- Code Assistance

Final approval always belongs to the engineer.

---

### Principle 3A — Engineering Judgment Is Never Replaced

**AI SHALL ASSIST ENGINEERS, NEVER REPLACE ENGINEERING JUDGMENT.**

- AI outputs are advisory.
- Engineering decisions require human responsibility.
- AI uncertainty must be visible.
- AI recommendations must be explainable and traceable.
- SATCO must never silently alter approved engineering decisions.

---

### Principle 4 — Customer Never Sees AI

Customers communicate with SATCO Company.

AI is an internal engineering tool.

---

### Principle 5 — Proactive AI

Whenever possible SATCO should:

- Analyze uploaded documents
- Detect missing information
- Identify engineering risks
- Prepare project summaries
- Suggest next engineering actions

without waiting for user questions.

---

### Principle 6 — Engineering Knowledge Belongs To SATCO

Every completed project improves the internal knowledge base.

Knowledge becomes reusable for future projects.

---

### Principle 7 — Modular Architecture

Every major component must remain independent.

Examples:

- CRM
- AI Brain
- Knowledge Base
- Prompt Engine
- PLC Assistant
- Document Analyzer

---

### Principle 8 — AI Provider Independence

The platform must never depend on a single AI provider.

OpenAI is currently the primary provider.

The architecture must support future AI providers.

---

### Principle 9 — Single Source Of Truth

PostgreSQL is the primary structured data source.

Engineering knowledge belongs to SATCO Platform.

---

### Principle 10 — Long-Term Goal

SATCO Platform is designed to scale from a single engineer to a complete engineering organization.

# Product Governance Principles

## Principle 1 — Engineering Judgment First

SATCO may assist engineering work but shall never replace engineering
judgment.

The engineer always remains responsible for every engineering decision.

---

## Principle 2 — AI Assists, Never Replaces

Artificial Intelligence within SATCO exists to support engineers.

AI may recommend, analyze, summarize, compare, explain, and identify risks.

AI shall never become the final engineering decision maker.

---

## Principle 3 — Solve Real Engineering Problems

Every capability introduced into SATCO must solve a real engineering
problem encountered during daily engineering work.

Features implemented only because they appear technically interesting
shall not be accepted.

---

## Principle 4 — Version 1 Before Expansion

Version 1 is built primarily for SATCO Engineering.

Future enterprise capabilities shall not delay or complicate Version 1.

Build depth before breadth.

---

## Principle 5 — Product Owner Approval

No new capability may enter SATCO Platform without explicit approval from
the Product Owner.

Ideas may be proposed freely.

Implementation requires Product Owner approval.

---

## Principle 6 — Complement, Never Replace

SATCO complements existing enterprise systems.

SATCO is not intended to replace ERP, CMMS, Primavera, SAP,
or existing engineering management platforms.

SATCO provides Engineering Intelligence alongside those systems.

---

## Principle 7 — Architecture Before Features

Every major capability shall first be reflected in:

- Constitution
- Architecture
- ADR
- Roadmap

before implementation begins.
