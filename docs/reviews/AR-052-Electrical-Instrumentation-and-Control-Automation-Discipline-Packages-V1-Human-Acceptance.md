## Accepted Architecture boundary

Acceptance preserves the reviewed operational-package architecture for exactly
Electrical, Instrumentation, and Control & Automation V1, including:

- trusted static source package registration and precompiled adapters;
- finite discipline-specific catalogs and deterministic bounded rules;
- consumption through existing authoritative owner workflows;
- minimal immutable package-origin provenance and exact centralized Audit
  operation identity;
- Engineering Identifier integration under accepted ADR-025;
- existing persistence plus a bounded provenance extension, subject to later
  separately accepted design and migration authority;
- intersection-only authorization and authorization-before-disclosure;
- fail-closed readiness and 39 package plus seven combined conformance vectors;
- three server-derived compiled discipline frontend components;
- all seven supported single/pair/integrated package combinations; and
- the five accepted batches without implementation detail expansion.

The accepted boundary excludes cross-discipline reasoning, standards
intelligence, Evidence Workbench expansion, Methods & Systems, product
completion, commercial authentication/security, entitlement enforcement,
deployment certification, procurement, maintenance, future disciplines,
runtime plugins, customer executable content, autonomous engineering approval,
control-system code generation, deep Control Systems Intelligence, and final
engineering calculations.
