# SATCO Engineering Intelligence Manifesto Architecture Review

**Document Reviewed:** `docs/Engineering_Intelligence_Manifesto.md`
**Manifesto Version:** 1.0
**Review Type:** Foundation Architecture Review
**Reviewer:** SATCO Platform Architecture Team
**Decision Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** PASS

## 1. Review Scope

The review evaluated:

- consistency with the SATCO Platform Constitution;
- consistency with Docs-First Architecture;
- consistency with the Governance Model;
- consistency with accepted platform architecture;
- consistency with existing Foundation Documents;
- absence of unauthorized implementation or PATCH scope.

## 2. Constitution Consistency

**PASS**

The Manifesto preserves Human engineering authority, advisory AI behavior,
modular architecture, provider independence, PostgreSQL structured-data
authority, organizational learning, and SATCO's long-term engineering mission.
It explicitly remains subordinate to the Constitution.

## 3. Docs-First Consistency

**PASS**

The Manifesto establishes enduring philosophy and decision criteria before
future roadmap, PATCH, design, or implementation activity. It creates no
implementation authorization and directs lower-level decisions through the
existing governance lifecycle.

## 4. Governance Consistency

**PASS**

The Manifesto has a defined authority, owner, scope, change process, and
relationship to the Constitution and Product Bible. Governance Model and
documentation-guide updates register it as the second Foundation layer without
elevating implementation or supporting records.

The Architecture Guardian review is PASS, and Product Owner approval is
recorded in the Foundation v1.2 Certification Record.

## 5. Architecture Consistency

**PASS**

The Manifesto preserves the approved Engineering Intelligence direction,
stable and extensible Engineering Knowledge Graph Core, modular extensions,
Organization boundaries, provider independence, and PostgreSQL authority. It
does not select technologies, redefine domain contracts, or bypass ADR
governance.

## 6. Existing Foundation Consistency

**PASS**

The Manifesto consolidates and elevates existing principles from the
Constitution, Engineering Philosophy, Product Vision, Product Principles,
Engineering Knowledge Model, Architecture, Experience Bible, and Governance
Model. No conflicting responsibility, product, architecture, experience, or
execution rule was identified.

## 7. Findings

No Critical, Major, or Minor findings remain.

## 8. Decision

**PASS**

SATCO Engineering Intelligence Manifesto v1.0 is consistent with the current
Foundation and is approved for registration as the second permanent Foundation
Document immediately after the Constitution.
