## Executive Assessment

EDS-020.2 is architecturally strong and substantially complete.

It successfully translates ADR-015 into:

- a bounded first-release Context taxonomy;
- independent Context dimensions;
- explicit ownership, stewardship, authority, review, maturity, freshness,
  criticality, source, conflict, missing-information, history, derivation, and
  snapshot contracts;
- minimum Human Review and Context Resolution Decision semantics;
- least-privilege authorization;
- cross-Workspace Interface Commitments without workflow;
- compatible search, audit, traceability, concurrency, and safe failure;
- an ordered eight-sub-patch roadmap.

One scope inconsistency prevents unconditional acceptance:

> PATCH-020 explicitly excludes AI generation from PATCH-020.2, while portions
> of the EDS authorize AI-assisted Context findings and place AI-generated
> Context creation inside PATCH-020.2.4.

The correction is narrow. The EDS may define how future or externally produced
AI-generated Context is classified, reviewed, read, and protected, and it may
provide the authorized read layer in PATCH-020.2.8. It may not authorize
PATCH-020.2 to generate AI findings.

## Authority, Review, Maturity, Freshness, and Criticality Review

### Authority

Authority changes require a governed source event, authorized Human Review, or
human Context Resolution Decision. Administrator role, ownership, confidence,
frequency, and recency cannot promote authority.

### Review

Review is version-, scope-, question-, evidence-, and reviewer-specific.
Completed evidence is retained. Review does not approve connected elements.

### Maturity

Maturity represents fitness development independently from authority, review,
freshness, and formal deliverable approval.

### Freshness

Freshness responds to source revision, decisive input, applicability, stage,
Interface Commitment, requirement, decision, correction, conflict, review-by
condition, evidence access, and steward uncertainty. No universal time-to-live
exists.

### Criticality

Criticality is consequence-based, contextual, explainable, and non-numeric.
It influences attention, review, freshness, snapshots, audit, and AI caution
without becoming authority.

These models are orthogonal and coherent.

## Human Authority Review

Human engineering responsibility is preserved.

The EDS distinguishes:

- information owner;
- source owner;
- engineering steward;
- reviewer;
- approver where separately governed;
- Project owner;
- Workspace owner;
- primary assignee;
- collaborator;
- administrator.

No role or ownership position automatically proves technical competence.
Explicit designation records responsibility without claiming to certify
professional qualification.

AI cannot review itself, verify facts, resolve conflicts, promote maturity,
fulfil material commitments, approve engineering work, or create human
decisions.

## AI-boundary Review

The behavioral safety boundary is sound:

- AI output remains advisory;
- evidence, assumptions, limitations, confidence basis, time, and provenance
  remain visible;
- material use requires Human Review;
- inaccessible sources remain inaccessible;
- AI cannot mutate Context or history.

The delivery boundary needs correction.

### Scope conflict

PATCH-020 defines PATCH-020.2 as:

- typed Context relationships;
- required and missing inputs;
- conflict and freshness semantics;
- minimum Decision Log entries;
- shared Human Review;

and explicitly excludes **AI generation**.

The EDS nevertheless says:

- PATCH-020.2 permits AI-assisted Context findings;
- AI-generated findings may be created and audited;
- PATCH-020.2.4 implements “Derived and AI-assisted Context” and
  AI-generated Context;
- scenario validation includes AI-assisted findings as PATCH-020.2 behavior.

This is implementation-scope leakage across an approved PATCH boundary.

### Required correction

The EDS should:

1. retain the AI-generated Context semantic model for compatibility and safe
   handling of future or externally supplied advisory records;
2. retain PATCH-020.2.8 as an authorized, bounded, read-only AI Context
   assembly layer;
3. rename PATCH-020.2.4 to **Derived Context**;
4. remove AI finding generation from PATCH-020.2.4 purpose and completion
   criteria;
5. remove creation of AI-generated findings from PATCH-020.2 audit scope,
   scenario completion claims, and current functional scope;
6. state that PATCH-020.2 accepts no AI-generated mutation path and creates no
   AI output;
7. defer AI-generated Context creation to PATCH-020.5 or another separately
   approved AI PATCH.

This correction does not require an architecture change. It restores the
accepted PATCH decomposition.

## Audit Review

Audit covers all material Context meanings, including:

- source, authority, maturity, review, freshness, criticality, conflict,

- failed and rejected mutation rollback;
- separation of generic audit from domain history.

After the AI scope correction, AI-generation events should be deferred with the

## Remaining Risks

- Context dimensions could be collapsed into convenient exclusive states
  during implementation.
- “Approved for Use” could be mistaken for formal deliverable approval despite
  explicit warnings.
- Steward designation could be mistaken for competence certification.
- Contextual source precedence could become hidden ordering.
- Criticality could drift into an unexplained universal score.
- Freshness could drift into one generic time threshold.
- Snapshot selection could retain excessive restricted evidence or omit
  decisive evidence.
- Interface Commitments could drift into tasks, schedules, or notifications.
- Derived findings could be promoted without preserving their original basis.
- Cross-Workspace search or summaries could leak inaccessible meaning.
- Minimum Context Resolution Decisions could expand into an unapproved full
  Decision Log.
- Performance validation datasets may not represent highly connected
  multidisciplinary Projects.

These are implementation and review risks already anticipated by the EDS.

## Remaining Ambiguities

- The organizational basis for designating competent stewards and reviewers is
  intentionally outside the platform’s current role model.
- Exact initial native references depend on which governed Project data and
  source domains exist when PATCH-020.2.1 begins.
- Deterministic derivations included in PATCH-020.2.4 remain to be bounded.
- The first explicit stage-boundary snapshot trigger remains to be approved.
- Representative Context density and relationship scale remain to be defined
  for performance validation.
- Existing retention rules bound the first release, but comprehensive
  retention and cross-Customer reuse require later governance.
- Specialized source-precedence rules require separate architecture approval.

These ambiguities are appropriately assigned to sub-patch evidence or future
governance and do not require additional permanent architecture.
